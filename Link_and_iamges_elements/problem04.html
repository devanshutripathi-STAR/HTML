Weekend Travel Guide: Jaipur

Jaipur, also known as the Pink City, is famous for its forts, palaces, traditional markets and regional food.

Attractions

Visitors can explore Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal and City Palace. These places represent the history and architecture of Jaipur.

Culture

Jaipur has a rich culture involving handicrafts, folk music, traditional clothing and local festivals.

Food

Popular food includes dal baati churma, ghevar, kachori and other Rajasthani dishes.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is generally recommended for sightseeing.

Travel Tips

Travel tip: Carry drinking water, protect yourself from the sun and confirm attraction timings before visiting.
