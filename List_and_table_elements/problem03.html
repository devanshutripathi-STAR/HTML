Preparation Before a Practical Class

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Review the relevant theory.
  3. Prepare the required materials.
  4. Reach the laboratory on time.
  5. Complete the practical work carefully.
  6. Submit the practical record.

Academic Rules

  • Maintain regular attendance.
  • Submit assignments before the deadline.
  • Carry your identity card.
  • Maintain discipline in classrooms.
  • Do not disturb other students.
  • Follow laboratory safety instructions.

Academic Definitions

Lecture
A teaching session focused on theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session conducted in a laboratory.
Tutorial
A session focused on practice and problem-solving.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the academic term.
Practical
A learning activity involving hands-on application.
