Registration Process

Registration Deadline: Check the official fest announcement.

  1. Choose your preferred competition.
  2. Read the eligibility and participation rules.
  3. Fill in the registration form.
  4. Pay the registration fee, if applicable.
  5. Submit the form before the deadline.
  6. Save the registration confirmation.

Fest Definitions

Open Event
An event open to participants who meet the specified conditions.
Team Event
A competition in which participants compete as a group.
Individual Event
A competition in which one participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of entering participant details for an event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing an event or activity.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition used to determine the result.

General Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry their college identity cards.
  • Participants must follow the instructions of coordinators.
  • Report to the venue before the scheduled time.
  • Maintain discipline and respect other participants.
  • Use college property responsibly.
  • Follow the rules of each competition.
  • Submit original work where required.
