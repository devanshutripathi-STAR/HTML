Science Notes

Chemistry

Water: H2O

Carbon dioxide: CO2

Oxygen: O2

Sulfuric acid: H2SO4

Ammonia: NH3

Atoms are the basic units of chemical elements.

Physics

Area is measured in m2.

Volume is measured in m3.

Speed = Distance / Time

Einstein's mass-energy relation: E = mc2

Force is commonly expressed as mass multiplied by acceleration.

Biology

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of living organisms.

DNA contains genetic information that helps organisms develop and function.

Quick Revision

Remember the difference between subscripts in chemical formulas and superscripts in mathematical and scientific expressions.
