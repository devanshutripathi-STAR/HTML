Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must maintain respectful behaviour toward teachers, staff members, and classmates.

Students should bring the required books and materials to every class.

Attendance

Students are expected to attend classes regularly and maintain the attendance requirement specified by the institution.

Attendance shortage may affect examination eligibility.

Examination

Students must follow all examination instructions and carry the required identification documents.

Assignment Submission

Assignments must be submitted before the announced deadline.
Late submissions may require permission from the concerned teacher.

Example deadline: 30 September 2026.

Discipline

Students should maintain discipline in classrooms, laboratories, and other college areas.
