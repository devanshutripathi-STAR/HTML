Financial Tips

  • Prepare a monthly budget.
  • Save a portion of your income.
  • Compare financial products carefully.
  • Protect your banking credentials.
  • Avoid sharing OTPs and passwords.
  • Understand loan terms before borrowing.

Account Opening Steps

  1. Select a suitable banking service.
  2. Read the terms and conditions.
  3. Submit the required information.
  4. Complete identity verification.
  5. Receive confirmation from the institution.

Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged or earned on a principal amount.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid towards a loan.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The duration for which a loan or investment is held.
