About the Technology and Career Expo

Introduction

The National Tech & Career Expo is an educational and professional event where students, job seekers, companies, educators and technology enthusiasts can interact.

The event includes career guidance, technical workshops, internship information, placement discussions and networking opportunities.

Event Preparation Tips

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Prepare technical and HR interview questions.
  • Carry a valid identity document.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interaction.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters and speakers.
  • Maintain professional communication.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive the event confirmation.
  3. Check in at the registration desk.
  4. Attend the opening session.
  5. Participate in workshops and discussions.
  6. Visit organization stalls.
  7. Network with speakers and professionals.
  8. Collect participation information and feedback.

Career Expo Terminology

Career Expo
An event where participants explore career and employment opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning and work experience in an organization.
Placement
A process through which an organization recruits a candidate.
Workshop
An interactive session focused on learning a specific skill.
Certification
Recognition provided after completing a course or activity.
Networking
Building professional relationships with people in a field.
