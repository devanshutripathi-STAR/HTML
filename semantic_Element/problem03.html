Appointment Process

  1. Review the available departments.
  2. Choose a preferred date and time.
  3. Complete the appointment request form.
  4. Wait for confirmation from the clinic.
  5. Follow the instructions provided by the clinic.

Important Terms

Consultation
A professional discussion with a healthcare provider.
Follow-up
A later consultation related to previous care.
OPD
Outpatient Department for patients who do not require admission.
Appointment
A scheduled time for a consultation or service.
